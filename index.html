Research

Pediatric Exclusivity and the Timing of Subsequent Generic Competition

Writing sample, 2026 · Forthcoming, Duke Journal of Economics

How much does pediatric exclusivity matter once a first generic has entered? Across 651 drug markets, I find that later generic approvals cluster at the legal deadline and shift six months under exclusivity, even though most of these applications were filed well before the patent expired.

Paper (PDF) Code and replication

Protest and Mobilization Against Gender-Based Violence: Evidence from Pakistan's Aurat March

Independent research paper, Duke University, 2025

I study whether Pakistan's Aurat March changed local protest against gender-based violence. Such protest was already declining in districts that later hosted the march; once I account for that decline, I find suggestive evidence that the march halted it.

Paper (PDF)

Intransitive Preferences: A Number-Theoretic Approach

Independent research paper, Duke University, 2025

Comparing options attribute by attribute can make choices cycle. Encoding each option's attributes as a product of primes, I show that standard utility, majority voting and Tversky's lexicographic semiorder differ only in how they weigh what each option lacks, and characterize when such rules stay transitive.

Extensions on Causally Inspired Out of Distribution Generalization

With Liam Connor, Hayden King and Vincent Li · Duke University, 2026

We survey two causal approaches to prediction in new domains, Invariant Risk Minimization and Causally Inspired Regularization, and propose two extensions of the latter.

Conditional Average Treatment Effect

With Allyson Cui, Sylvia Vincent and Ethan Ouellette · COMPSCI 675, Duke University

Building on TARNet, we estimate heterogeneous treatment effects on synthetic and semi-synthetic medical data with controlled confounding, and find that estimated propensity scores and a balancing penalty improve accuracy.

Paper (PDF)

How the Olympic Games Shape Global Sports Attention: A YouTube Trending Analysis

With Alex Hirsh-de Ruiter and Hayden King · Duke University

Using 2.9 million YouTube Trending records from 11 countries, we find that the share of sports videos rises by about 14% during the Summer Olympics, while a country's medal count shows no consistent link to sports trending.
